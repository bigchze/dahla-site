DAHLA would like to encourage everyone to take advantage of the upcoming Visit Durango & Tourism Advisory Commission Working Groups. These meetings will take place every other month and are intended to create a space for community and stakeholder feedback on Visit Durango's strategic tourism initiatives. The goal is for these discussions to result in actionable items for staff and advocacy opportunities for working group participants.

Visit Durango believes these working groups are an important opportunity for the lodging community to be directly involved in the conversation. If we have concerns about occupancy, visitor numbers, marketing efforts, or the data being used to measure our tourism economy, this is an opportunity to bring those concerns forward and help shape the discussion. DAHLA strongly encourages as many lodgers as possible to participate and make sure our industry has a strong voice at the table.

To RSVP, please email [JJ Desrosiers](mailto:jj.desrosiers@durangoco.gov) and [Rachel Welsh](mailto:Rachel.welsh@durangoco.gov) with the working group(s) you would like to attend.
