Purgatory Resort's winter ski season is a major demand driver for Durango-area lodging, with average annual snowfall of roughly 260 inches across more than 1,600 skiable acres, multiple terrain parks, and Colorado's largest snowcat skiing operation. The resort typically operates from late November through early April, with the heaviest visitor traffic around the winter holidays, MLK weekend, and Presidents' Day weekend.

Beyond the holiday peaks, Purgatory's terrain and family-friendly reputation bring steady weekend and midweek traffic through the rest of the season, making winter an important shoulder-season revenue window for DAHLA members. Properties should coordinate with the resort's published operating calendar and event schedule when planning winter staffing.
